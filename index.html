How to play

Left-drag draws, right-drag erases.
Player: ← → move, ↑ jump, X or Space shoot; its head absorbs what it touches.
In 3D: W / S walk into and out of the box. Drag outside the box (or middle-drag, Q E) to turn it; the wheel zooms; [ ] move the slice.
P pauses.
